Description

Grace Park Heights has proved to be a very popular development since it was built in the 70’s due in part to its central location near the city. This cul de sac development is ideal for young families who move in then stay for the convenience. In common with previous sales, number 233 is an executor’s sale of a nice family home in good condition. It has been upgraded over the years with additional insulation, modern windows and heating so you can comfortably move straight in if you are lucky enough to secure it.
